Anonymise your documents without them ever leaving your computer

Sine Nomine replaces the names of people, companies, products and places, as well as sensitive information (emails, phone numbers, IBANs, addresses, websites…), in your Word, PowerPoint, Excel, PDF, Markdown, CSV, HTML and text files. Everything happens on your machine: no data is sent over the Internet.

Download version 0.1.4.0 How it works

Windows 10 or 11 · evaluation version with an access key · documents in French and English · bilingual interface

100% local

Your files stay on your disk. The application uses the Internet only while it is being installed, to download the detection models.

Two modes

Pseudonymise: random codes (Personne_KQXMB) with an archive so the names can be recovered later. Anonymise: Personne_1, keeping nothing.

You stay in control

Before anything is replaced, you review each detection, tab by tab, with the context of each word. Original files are never modified.

Reversible

A pseudonymised file can be restored to its original state at any time using the code archive, and pseudonyms can be corrected afterwards.

How it works

  1. Drop your files or point to a folder. PDFs are read, including scanned documents (optical character recognition).
  2. Review the detections: people, companies, products, places, sensitive information. Untick, correct, merge duplicates, add anything missed.
  3. Confirm: Sine Nomine produces new files, with the file names themselves anonymised.
  4. Collect the result and its detailed report (HTML and Excel): what was replaced, where, and what was kept.

What is detected

Names

People (including particles and titles: “Charles de Gaulle”, “Dr Martin”), companies, products and brands, cities and countries. Accents, capitalisation and foreign spellings are recognised (“Léa” = “Lea”, “Kärscher” = “Kaerscher”).

Contact details and identifiers

Emails, phone numbers (France, UK, US, international), addresses, IBANs and BICs, bank cards, French social security numbers, SIRET, VAT, number plates, passports, NINO, SSN, NHS.

Digital traces

URLs and domain names, IP and MAC addresses, accounts (@name), file and network paths, GPS coordinates, Office file metadata (author, company, comments).

Before you use it

No automatic detection is 100% reliable. Names may be missed, or detected wrongly. Human review of every run is essential, and the result must be proofread before it is shared. Pseudonymisation is not anonymisation within the meaning of the GDPR; even anonymisation does not guarantee that a text cannot be identified by cross-referencing.
  • Runs on Windows 10 or 11. Installation downloads about 2 GB of models and needs about 6 GB of disk space.
  • Evaluation version: an access key, to be requested from contact@sinenomine.app, is activated during installation; it sets the period of use and the number of installations (terms of use).
  • Documents in German, Spanish and Italian are recognised but analysed less thoroughly; other alphabets are not supported.
