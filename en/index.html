Local processing is at the heart of Sine Nomine

Pseudonymisation and restoration of your data take place entirely on your computer.

  • Sine Nomine does not send your documents to a remote service to pseudonymise them or to restore the original values.
  • The correspondence table between pseudonyms and original values stays on your computer.
  • The Internet is used only for installation: downloading components and detection models, and activating the access key.
  • Exchanges with an AI or any other online service are your decision and your action: Sine Nomine sends nothing on your behalf.

How it works: three steps

  1. Pseudonymise your documents locally.
    Sine Nomine replaces the values to protect with pseudonyms and keeps their correspondence on your computer.
  2. Use the services of your choice.
    You send the pseudonymised files yourself to an AI or another online service.
  3. Get your original data back.
    You retrieve the pseudonymised result and use Sine Nomine to restore the original values and obtain a readable document.

Please note: before sending a file, check the information and contextual clues it still contains (job title, place, event, combination of details): they can allow a person or organisation to be recognised even without their name.

Built for real case files

Your own terms

You supply the list of names that must disappear (clients, cases, employees): they are searched for in all their spellings, even if automatic detection does not recognise them.

Whole folders

A batch can hold many files: the same name receives the same pseudonym from one document to the next.

A table under lock

The correspondence table is kept in an encrypted vault on your computer: names and sensitive values never appear there in clear text.

Revisions and comments

In Office files, the text of tracked changes and comments is processed, and their authors are masked.

Images and unanalysed content

An image is never declared anonymised: it is kept and flagged, or removed. In reinforced confidentiality mode, content that can be neither processed nor removed blocks publication of the file.

Formats

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Markdown, CSV, HTML and text; PDFs are read as input (scanned pages need the character-recognition component, whose result must be checked closely). In Excel, sheet names, formulas and pivot tables are not processed, and Sine Nomine says so.

Before you use it

No automatic detection is 100% reliable. Names can escape the tool or be detected wrongly. Human review of every run is essential, and the result must be checked before it is shared. Pseudonymisation is not anonymisation within the meaning of the GDPR; even anonymisation does not guarantee that a text cannot be identified by cross-referencing.
