Before you buy

What is Sine Nomine?

Sine Nomine is Windows software that replaces, on your computer, the names and other sensitive information in your documents with pseudonyms, then restores the original values in the results you get back. It is used to prepare your documents before using them with an artificial intelligence or an online service. It is published by Habeas Corpus.

What is the difference between the Standard and Expert editions?

The Standard edition (€49 incl. VAT) is intended for non-professional use: it includes the core of Sine Nomine, with a single project. The Expert edition is intended for professional use, including freelancers and regulated professions: it adds multiple projects, Document and Client scopes, settings profiles, additional languages, date generalisation, PDF export and multi-seat licences. Launch price: €149 per seat, excluding VAT, through 31 March 2027. From 1 April 2027: €199 per seat, excluding VAT. Either way, the licence has no time limit and includes all future versions. Details are on the purchase page.

Is the licence a subscription?

No. It is a one-off purchase, with no subscription. The licence is valid with no time limit on each computer for which it was acquired.

Can I transfer my licence to a new computer?

No. The licence is non-transferable: any change of computer requires the purchase of a new licence.

Can I install Sine Nomine on several computers?

One licence covers one computer. To equip several, a business orders the Expert edition and enters the number of seats (1 to 100): it receives a single key that allows as many installations, and the application shows the remaining credits at each installation. The Standard edition is limited to one computer.

Install and activate

Where do I find my access key?

After a purchase, the key is sent to the address entered at payment, in an email titled "Your Sine Nomine licence". If you cannot find it, check your spam folder, then write to contact@sinenomine.app giving the address used to pay. See the Install page.

What computer do I need?

64-bit Windows 10 or 11, 8 GB of memory or more recommended, about 6 GB of disk space. The program to download is small; about 2 GB of components are fetched on first launch.

Does the application need the Internet?

Only for installation: downloading components and detection models (about 2 GB) and activating the access key. Once installed, Sine Nomine works offline. Exchanges with an AI are carried out by you, outside Sine Nomine.

How do I know the installation succeeded?

You double-click lancer.bat: a black window opens and must stay open. The first installation is long (about 2 GB to download). Sine Nomine is ready when your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765/. On later starts, allow 20 to 60 seconds. See the steps.

Using Sine Nomine with an AI

How do I use pseudonymised documents with an AI?

  1. Pseudonymise your documents with Sine Nomine, on your computer.
  2. Review the files produced: information or contextual clues may still allow identification.
  3. Send the pseudonymised files yourself to the AI or service of your choice (ChatGPT, Mistral, Claude or another). Sine Nomine is not affiliated with these services and sends nothing on your behalf.
  4. Retrieve the result yourself, then restore the original values with Sine Nomine.

Which document formats are supported?

Word, PowerPoint, Excel, text, Markdown, CSV and HTML; PDFs are read as input, including scanned ones. Each format has its limits (Excel formulas, PDF layout, images): see the formats table.

How many projects and pseudonyms can I use?

11,881,376 pseudonyms available per project; multiple projects with the Expert edition.

Confidentiality and restoration

Are my documents sent to Sine Nomine or its publisher?

No. Sine Nomine processes your files only on your computer; the publisher receives neither your documents nor the names they contain. The only communication with the publisher is the activation of the access key (the key, a random installation identifier, the version and the language). If you place your files in a synchronisation service or send them to an online service, that service receives them, by your own choice.

What is the difference between pseudonymisation and anonymisation?

Pseudonymisation replaces values with codes and keeps a correspondence table, so the original values can be recovered. Anonymisation replaces values without keeping a table: Sine Nomine keeps no means of recovering the original values. Under the GDPR, pseudonymised data remain personal data, and an anonymised text can remain identifiable by cross-referencing.

How do I restore the original values?

You give Sine Nomine the pseudonymised result you retrieved. The application replaces the pseudonyms with the original values using the correspondence table kept on your computer, and you obtain a readable document. Always review the result.
