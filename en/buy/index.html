What you are buying

Sine Nomine pseudonymises your documents on your computer so that you can use them with an AI or an online service, then restores the original values in the results. Pseudonymisation and restoration take place entirely locally; the correspondence table stays on your computer. 11,881,376 pseudonyms available per project; multiple projects with the Expert edition. After payment: confirmation, licence and installation

  1. Confirmation. Once our server has verified the payment, your access key (the licence) is sent to the email address you entered when paying. It is never shown in a page address.
  2. Download. Go to the Download page, download the archive and check its fingerprint.
  3. Installation. During installation, enter your access key: it is activated with an Internet connection, one activation per computer (the application shows the remaining credits). After that, Sine Nomine works offline.

If the payment is refused or cancelled, no key is issued. If you have received nothing after a successful payment, write to contact@sinenomine.app with the address used to pay.
